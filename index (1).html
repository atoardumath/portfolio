About Me

I am a Mathematics student at the University of Dhaka, currently progressing toward my fourth year. I have a strong interest in mathematics, particularly areas such as Real Analysis, Abstract Algebra, Multivariable Calculus, Probability, Optimization, Fourier Analysis, and Numerical Methods.

I am focused on my academic development and regularly prepare for university examinations by working through mathematical proofs, definitions, examples, and problem-solving techniques. I prefer explanations that are clear, step-by-step, and easy to understand, especially when a mathematical concept is difficult at first. I also like exam-oriented answers that are concise but contain enough reasoning to earn full marks.

Alongside pure mathematics, I have experience working with computational and numerical methods, including Fortran and MATLAB. I am interested in understanding not only mathematical formulas but also how those formulas can be implemented computationally.

Looking ahead, I am interested in building a career in teaching and education. I particularly like the idea of becoming a mathematics teacher or lecturer, where I can teach students, develop batches, earn a good income, and use part of my earnings to help people who are less fortunate.

Overall, I would describe myself as a mathematics-focused student who values clear understanding, practical problem solving, academic growth, and using education to make a positive contribution to society.
